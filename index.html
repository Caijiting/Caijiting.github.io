Jiting Cai | 蔡霁廷

I am a Master's student in Robotics (MSR) at Carnegie Mellon University's Robotics Institute, working with Prof. Wenshan Wang and Prof. Sebastian Scherer at the AirLab.

Previously, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I worked with Prof. Yong-Lu Li and Prof. Cewu Lu on visual reasoning.

I was also fortunate to spend summer internships at UMass Amherst and the MIT-IBM Watson AI Lab, working closely with Prof. Chuang Gan on simulated world generation and robot manipulation.

My research interests lie at the intersection of computer vision, robotics, and multimodal learning. I am particularly interested in robot perception, including SLAM and dense correspondence, multimodal representation learning, and world models for robotics.
